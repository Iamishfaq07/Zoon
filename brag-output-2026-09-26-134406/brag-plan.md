# /brag plan: Zoon, the intelligence cut

## What it is
An iPhone and Apple Watch app that reads the night your Watch records and explains it with on-device intelligence. It shows sleep stages and the shortfall against your own need, has a Coach running on Apple Intelligence that answers from your own nights, and keeps an evidence ledger that tests what works for you. It maps your day and puts a plan on your wrist. No account, no server.

## Who it's for
Apple Watch owners who get a number every morning and still wake up tired.

## What sets it apart
The intelligence is personal and honest:
- it compares you with your own baseline
- it runs on your iPhone
- it ranks every claim by how it was found (suspected, associated, tested)
- it says "a pattern, not proof" when it cannot prove a cause

## Most impressive claim
An AI sleep coach that learns what works for you, without your data ever leaving your iPhone and Apple Watch.

## Visual hook
"You slept 7h 32m." followed by "So why are you tired?"

- **Screens:** Today (`docs/screenshots/hires/today.jpg`), Coach (`coach.jpg`), and the Watch Tonight moon face (`watch/hires/page-tonight.jpg`).

- **Body-clock markers:** from the app's Body Clock screen.
- **Coach:** runs on Apple Intelligence on device (`CoachChat`).

## Tone
`polished`, with more information than the short cut. Calm night palette, and a violet-to-green gradient for the words that carry the AI idea.

## Sound
No musical instruments. Night wind and crickets, a whoosh on each cut, a rising airy swell under the neural-network scene, quiet key taps under the typed answer, and birdsong at dawn.
