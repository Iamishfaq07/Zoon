# /brag plan: Zoon

## What it is
An iPhone and Apple Watch app that reads the sleep your Watch already records and tells you what the night meant: stages, shortfall against your own need, a plan for tonight, and a Coach that answers from your own nights. On device, no account.

## Who it's for
People with an Apple Watch who get a number every morning ("7h 32m") and still wake up tired.

## What sets it apart
It explains instead of scoring. It compares you with your own baseline, never a crowd, and says "a pattern, not proof" when it cannot prove a cause. Everything stays on the phone and the watch.

## Most impressive claim
Your whole night, and what to do about it tonight, on your wrist, worked out on your own devices.

## Visual hook
A single number most people already know from their Watch, "You slept 7h 32m.", followed by the question it never answers: "So why are you tired?"

## Tone
`polished` with a warm, calm night feel: long holds, soft fades, no hard cuts. Palette from the app: navy night (#0e1128 to #05060d), violet (#9e8ff2 / #7b61ff), amber shortfall (#f5a25d), calm green (#3fd6a0). Type: Nunito (rounded, the nearest web face to SF Pro Rounded) for headlines, Manrope for text.

## Sound
No musical instruments (owner's request). A nature soundscape written as one piece: night wind and crickets, a soft whoosh on each cut, a few quiet key taps under the typed Coach answer, then birdsong as the sky turns to dawn on the outro.

## Share caption
Your Watch says you slept 7h 32m. Zoon tells you why you're still tired, and what to do tonight. On your iPhone and Apple Watch, with no account and no server.
